# Course Platform

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Purchases and refunds

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask to get back what they paid for one course purchase. A person on the team approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancel my purchase, cancellation, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their refund request is approved. A refund ends the learner's access to that course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

**Chargeback**:
Reserved for a payment the buyer's bank forcibly reverses, without asking us first. This is a different concept from a refund request, and the platform doesn't model it yet.
